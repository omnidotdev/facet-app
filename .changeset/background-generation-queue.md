AI generation now runs in the background. Generate queues your part and frees the panel right away, so you can keep modeling, close the panel, or even leave the page while it generates. A queue in the "Describe a part" panel shows each part as Queued, Generating, Ready, or Failed, and the button shows how many are generating or ready. Open a ready part to load it into a new model tab (it is still checked and refined as before), retry a failed one, or discard what you do not need. A short message lets you know when a part finishes while you are elsewhere in the app. Ready parts wait for up to 24 hours. Generating with your own Synapse key works as before.
