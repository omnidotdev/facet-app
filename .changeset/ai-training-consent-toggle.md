Add an AI training-data opt-in to the account menu. A signed-in user who can use managed generation sees a clearly labeled, off-by-default switch to share their prompts and generated models so Facet's AI can keep improving, with a plain-language explanation and a link to the privacy policy. Turning it on is a single affirmative action; turning it off stops collection and deletes what was shared. The toggle reflects and updates the gateway's record of the user's consent.
