Edges stay visible on dark part colors. In the shaded-with-edges display, a part colored navy, black, or another dark color now gets light edge lines, where the dark lines used to disappear into it.

The 3D view no longer goes blank while you drag the divider between the code and the view, or resize the window. It keeps drawing the part the whole time.
