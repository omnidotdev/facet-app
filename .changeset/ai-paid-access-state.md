Managed AI generation is now for paying customers: Facet Pro, or a workspace that has bought Omni credits (Facet Free no longer includes managed credits). Signed-in users who have not paid yet now see the Describe a part panel in a paid-access state that explains AI generation uses Omni credits and offers "Buy credits", "Upgrade to Pro", and "Use your own Synapse key", so you can still generate with your own key. After you buy credits or upgrade and return to the tab, the panel switches to generating on its own. A generation refused because you have not paid yet now opens that same state instead of saying you are out of credits.
