Change a part by chatting with it. A chat docked under the 3D view takes plain requests like "make the ring thicker" and updates the open model in the same tab, so each request builds on the last. Your slider settings carry over into the updated model, a change that does not build is repaired or left out, and Undo puts the part and its sliders back as they were. Changes use AI credits like Describe a part, or your own Synapse key.
